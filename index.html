About

I’m Aarav, and I like to understand how things work by building them myself. I started out making Swift apps, then got pulled into machine learning and haven’t looked back.

These days I train small language models from scratch. I began by counting character pairs, built a neural network by hand, then wrote a GPT with self-attention. Keeping the models small means I can see every moving part, and I’d rather fully understand a tiny model than call a big one through an API.

I work mostly in Python and Jupyter, with a few Swift apps along the way. Next, I want to make my models bigger and better.
